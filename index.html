Sonora — YouTube Music without the music videos

A full music client that refuses to show you music videos. It searches YouTube Music’s song index, filters out anything that smells like a video, and plays audio-only releases. Playlists, saved albums and downloads live in your browser — no account.

  • Audio-only releases
  • Playlists & saved albums
  • Download bitrate 48–256 kbps
  • Installs to your Home Screen
